# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Users

Vietnamese learners studying English or Japanese.

## Product Purpose

SuperLanguage supports language learning through structured lessons, vocabulary study, and AI-assisted conversation practice. Success means learners can find what to study or review and begin practicing with less friction.

## Positioning

The product brings learning paths, vocabulary lookup and review, and AI conversation practice together in one English and Japanese learning experience.

## Operating Context

Learners use the web app to browse learning paths and topics, study lessons, look up and save vocabulary topics, review words, and practice conversations with AI. Signed-in users can track learning progress and conversation history.

## Capabilities and Constraints

- Supports English and Japanese learning.
- Existing lesson, vocabulary, review, conversation, and voice-recording behavior and data flows must remain intact during the requested visual redesign.
- The frontend is a Vite application using HTML, TypeScript, and CSS.

## Brand Commitments

- Preserve the SuperLanguage product name and existing logo asset.
- Keep learner-facing copy in Vietnamese where the existing interface uses Vietnamese.

## Evidence on Hand

- `README.md` documents the learning platform and learner capabilities.
- `Frontend/src/pages/learning/index.html`, `Frontend/src/pages/vocabulary/index.html`, and `Frontend/src/pages/chat/index.html` are the existing learner surfaces.

## Product Principles

- Keep the next useful learning action easy to find.
- Make vocabulary lookup and review straightforward.
- Keep AI conversation focused on language practice.
- Preserve existing learner data and behavior while improving presentation.
- Make the learner experience usable across desktop and mobile screens.
